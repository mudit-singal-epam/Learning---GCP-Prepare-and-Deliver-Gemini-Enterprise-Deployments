# Lead with Day 1 value

## **The Activate phase**

This is the part of the change management process where the Activate phase begins.

Gemini Enterprise provides a lot of value as soon as the first data connectors are established, but the core capabilities are available straight out-of-the-box. Let's explore some of those core capabilities.

- **AI Assistant**

  Generate unit test cases for this [code]
- **Web search**

  Show me recent news about [My Company]
- **Media generation**

  I have a new product which does [X]. Create an image for the website.
- **NotebookLM**

  Get answers and summaries from curated sets of content
- **Deep Research**

  Research the impact of AI on customer service.
- **Idea Generation**

  Brainstorm a gamified employee onboarding process
- **Agent Designer**

  Create custom agents without code
- **Custom Agents**

  Deploy and govern custom agents from any framework including ADK into Gemini Enterprise
- **Chrome Integration**

  Invoke Gemini Enterprise directly from the Chrome omnibox

There are some starter prompt ideas, but encourage your customer to be creative and create their own!

There are some tips for effective prompts in Gemini Enterprise here: [Prompt Guide](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise/resources/prompt-guide?e=48754805&hl=en#chapter-0)

## **Focus on Day-1 value**

It's important to think ahead and plan for future engagements, but in your first engagement you still need to deliver value right away. You can build momentum and customer excitement by showcasing immediate, out-of-the-box capabilities from Day-1.

- **Access world-class Gemini models**

  Gain instant access to Google's cutting-edge Gemini models. Use the latest multimodal breakthrough of Google's best AI technology to solve your most complex business problems.
- **Empower everyone with an agentic platform**

  Chat with Gemini to search and analyze information, and orchestrate agents to automate workflows. With no-code tools, any user—from marketing to finance—can build their own custom agents.
- **Put a taskforce of AI agents to work**

  Enjoy a suite of Google-made agents—like Deep Research, NotebookLM, and Coding Agents— delivering value from day one. Also easily add your own custom agents or third-party agents.
- **Ground agents in your business reality**

  Securely connect to your company's data wherever it lives—in Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, business apps like Salesforce and SAP, or datastores like BigQuery—to give your agents relevant context.
- **Deploy and manage with confidence**

  Centrally visualize, secure, audit and govern all your agents with Gemini Enterprise, helping you to meet the security, compliance, and sovereignty requirements of your organization.
- **Leverage the rich agentic AI partner ecosystem**

  Automate cross-platform workflows using built-in connectors to partner apps, accelerate your AI journey with service partners, and find innovative partner solutions at the agent marketplace.

## **Web Search**

For the most up-to-date info, Gemini Enterprise can use Google public web search results as an answer source.

On the Gemini Enterprise landing page, enable or disable Web Search by clicking on Sources, and then the toggle button next to each source.

Google Search Results is enabled by default if allowed by admin.

Simply paste a public URL and start a conversation with its content.

> Note: Some websites may not work as some publishers block access to their content.

Ask a question where the answer requires recent information like:

- Summarize the latest news on Google Cloud
- Who are the competitors of Google Cloud?

All generated answers' display their sources, which can include public websites.

Ask follow up questions for any search results.

Highlight a specific section and ask a specific question on that topic.

## **Media generation**

Generate images and videos using Google’s Imagen and Veo models.

Generate an image using their company as an example, or describe Gemini Enterprise.

Use the prompt chips or select the image generator tool. When selecting the tool, it's not necessary to specifically ask the model to create an image.

For example: “I have a new product which can search across 100+ data sources and help me analyze blended data and create agents on this blended data. Generate an image for its website.”

When the response comes back, copy or download the image.

Additionally, generate videos up to 8 seconds long using the prompt chips, or the **Generate a video** tool. Videos can also be downloaded for offline use.

## **NotebookLM**

Embedded directly within Gemini Enterprise, a compliant NotebookLM provides answers and summaries from custom sets of content.

NotebookLM is in the left navigation menu under the Agents tab. Any agents can be pinned, including NotebookLM, which will make them accessible in the left navigation menu.

Create a new Notebook

Import some content.

In this example, there is an [RFP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eLt9gNrh9V1kU_wYBXW2Ewvd3xAsCXpHGYdjJy8oyRg/edit?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-bBKXuH3B51gvP3uEBnyv4Q) document, and another that outlines the [receiving company’s capabilities](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y-J8ioV8EiHf6WKP9d34cDJ20UETtx7HgRntx-HbB6w/edit?usp=sharing).

First, ask NotebookLM to, “Summarize the key requirements, evaluation criteria, and deadlines outlined in the RFP”

When the answer comes back, click on a citation to view the specific source content for that point.

Ask additional questions like, “Identify all mandatory requirements in the RFP and cross-reference them with the product capabilities. Note any gaps.”

## **Deep Research**

Get in-depth reports grounded in web research.

Select the Deep Research agent under the Agent tab to:

- Select data sources for research
- Review and edit research plan before execution
- Read and copy multi-page reports including citations
- Listen to audio summary of the report

Give Gemini Enterprise a research topic, for example:

“Research the impact of AI on customer service”

Or…

“Summarize the primary marketing channels and messaging themes used by [Competitor X] and [Competitor Y] in the '[New Market Segment, Mobile phones]'."

Review the research plan and ask Gemini Enterprise to make any edits, or, click “Start Research.”

Wait a few minutes as Gemini Enterprise searches hundreds of sources to answers its questions, coming up with new ones and refining its plan along the way.

When Research is complete, note the cited report and play the Podcast-style audio summary.

## **Idea Generation**

Agents can continuously generate ideas on a topic, run multi-angle evaluation, and rank them, tournament-style.

Select the Idea Generation agent under the Agent tab.

Then give the agent a topic to ideate around.

For example: “Brainstorm a gamified employee onboarding process”.

Review the tournament plan and ask Gemini Enterprise to make any edits, or, click “Start Session.”

Click into the newly-created tournament details.

It may take a few minutes for ideas to start generating.

Once they do, review the details of each idea…

…and watch as the tournament ranking evolves.

## **Admin dashboard**

In Gemini Enterprise, there is a unified admin experience to orchestrate set-up activities.

There is a central dashboard that guides administrators toward an initial day 1 value experience through web search, first-party Agents, content creation, and much, much more.

It also guides the administrator with onboarding next steps, such as WIF setup, IAM permissions, and license management.
