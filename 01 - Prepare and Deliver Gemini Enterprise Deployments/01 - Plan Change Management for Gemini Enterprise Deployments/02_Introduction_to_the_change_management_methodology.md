# Introduction to the change management methodology

## **Every deployment has three dimensions:**
**People, Process, and Technology**

You already know the technology, and you probably know how it will improve people's lives. Your mission is to bring everyone together, understand what they do, and show them how the technology solves their challenges.

**Work transformation**

When People, Process, and Technology align, that is where you actually transform things for the business.

## **People don’t like change**

The trouble is getting people on board with the change. Most people are naturally resistant to change in one way or another. According to Everett Rogers only 16% of people adapt easily to change. That means that the remaining 84% will need your support along the way.

Fortunately people react in fairly predictable ways to change, even if the change is expected. Eventually they accept the change, begin experimenting, and integrating it into their life. The goal of change management is to remove the fear and uncertainty through well-defined communication. You can then alleviate the frustration through training, and help the user embrace the change.

## **Embrace change**

Effectively managing change means that you helped people by addressing their fears and misconceptions, studied their processes and developed solutions for their challenges, and trained them how to get the most out of the technology.

## **Start small, then spread through success**

One way to address this is to start by winning over a few key people. Then spreading to engage more people in the organization, with your original successes able to be used as case studies.

## **The change journey**

Change management isn't an isolated process, it begins within the sales cycle but continues into delivery.

The Gemini Enterprise change management methodology will help you to address the challenges around change management. The change journey to long-term success begins with preparation and is then followed by four key phases - Excite, Activate, Expand, and Transform.
Each phase is crucial for setting up your customers for success, so make sure you don’t discount early preparation and communication, or analyzing success metrics long after go-live.

- **Prepare:**

  - Preparation is key to the success of your change management journey.

  - You start by reviewing the project resources Google has prepared for you in the Partner Advantage Portal and Delivery Navigator.

  - You identify the available resources that will support and facilitate you and your customers organization along the change management journey, and plan the following phases of engagement.

- **Excite:**

  - In the Excite phase, you find and engage with customer sponsors, gain sponsorship, align sponsors, and build excitement for the change.

  - You will also support your customer in analyzing their organizations needs, identifying Gemini Enterprise use cases for their organization and determining which ones to tackle, identifying user groups, outlining the scope of the work, and assessing organizational impacts.

- **Activate:**

  - In the Activate phase, you will demonstrate the value that your customer can unlock from day-1.

  - Gemini Enterprise provides a lot of value out-of-the-box, and even more once the first data connectors are established.

  - It also offers a value-driven roadmap towards advanced capabilities.

  - You will help your customer get hands-on with the core capabilities by prototyping and iterating on useful queries to share, developing no-code agents, and understanding the possibilities with advanced agents and connectors.

- **Expand:**

  - In the Expand phase, it's all about communicating the top-down visions and bottom-up successes to grow adoption, and managing support operations.

  - As part of this phase, you will help your customer promote adoption, prioritize transformation, develop a communications plan, and combine high-level strategic planning with practical input from teams who will use the technology.

- **Transform:**

  - In the Transform phase, you support your customer in planning and running training workshops, measuring adoption, promoting success stories, and providing self-help resources.

  - The Transform phase incorporates championing innovation, measuring and evaluating success, promoting innovation initiatives, and training on new releases.
