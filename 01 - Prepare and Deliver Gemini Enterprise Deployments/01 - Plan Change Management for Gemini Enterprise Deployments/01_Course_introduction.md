# Course introduction


Welcome to the course **Plan Change Management for Gemini Enterprise Deployments**.

This course will guide you through designing customer engagements that result in successful, well-utilized Gemini Enterprise deployments. Study the art of change management, how to identify and engage sponsors, and recruit early adopters. Help your customer identify and address cultural change challenges and skills gaps. Effectively deliver project communications. Learn how to support your customer to plan, offer, conduct, and evaluate end-user training. Additionally, leverage partner resources, and create essential project documents.

This course is designed for Google and Partner presales teams, project management, and team members delivering change management elements of solutions and are tasked with:

- Positioning the value of change management.
- Managing cultural change and skill gaps of customers adopting Gemini Enterprise.
- Engaging sponsors and enlisting the support of customer stakeholders.
- Developing communications and training plans to help grow adoption of the platform within an organization.

You should have a basic understanding of change management and the impact change has on organizations and their users, be able to communicate effectively with end users and key stakeholders, and have a general knowledge of the process and resources needed to deliver end-user training.

In this course you will learn how to:

**✓**Describe the Gemini Enterprise change management methodology.

**✓**Engage sponsors and recruit early adopters to provide support during the project.

**✓**Identify cultural change challenges and address skills gaps.

**✓**Showcase immediate out-of-the-box capabilities from Day 1.

**✓**Explain how to most effectively deliver communications about the project.

**✓**Explain how to plan, offer, conduct, and evaluate the results of end-user training.
